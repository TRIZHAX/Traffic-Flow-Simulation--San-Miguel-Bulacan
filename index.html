Computational model

Traffic density k = N / L vehicles per kilometre of directional road
Average speed v̄ = Σvᵢ / N mean of all vehicle speeds
Travel delay D = Tactual − Tfree actual minus free-flow travel time
Effective capacity C = Cbase × fweather × fcondition lane-metres reduced by weather and road state
Volume / capacity x = N / C x<1 free flow · x≈1 unstable · x>1 congestion
Car following (IDM) a = a₀[1 − (v/v₀)⁴ − (s*/s)²] s* = s₀ + vT + vΔv / 2√(a₀b)

Data source

Road geometry, intersections, road names, rivers and landmarks are extracted from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) for San Miguel, Bulacan. Every road segment carries an assigned traffic direction; the directed network is verified strongly connected, so vehicles can always reach a destination without driving against traffic.
